2.8 combat foley additions (14 recordings, all CC0):
Kenney RPG Audio: knife slices, drawing a blade, cloth motion, chop and pouch handling.
https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio
Mirror: https://github.com/Boyquotes/kenney-rpg-audio-for-godot
Kenney Impact Sounds: heavy metal, heavy punches, glass, plate and soft impacts.
https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
Pitch variation, attack-specific layering and party distance attenuation are applied at playback.
The UI switch recording is no longer used as a weapon swing.

2.12 combat voices (21 short, nonverbal attack takes):
Brandon Song (wolfwoot), Voice Clip Pack - Male Adventurer RPG, CC0.
https://opengameart.org/content/voice-clip-pack-male-adventurer-rpg
cicifyre, Female RPG Voice Starter Pack (three voice tones), CC0.
https://opengameart.org/content/female-rpg-voice-starter-pack
Only attack effort recordings are used, not spoken English spell names or death screams.
Processing: mono, noise-band filtering, silence margins, constant gain/short edge fades,
Ogg Vorbis encoding. Original voice pitch is preserved during playback.
These are licensed human recordings, not cloned or synthesized voices.

Boss music: Juhani Junkala / SubspaceAudio, Epic Boss Battle [Seamlessly Looping], CC0.
https://opengameart.org/content/boss-battle-music
Processing: constant gain 0.7, Ogg Vorbis encoding. Source loop length is retained.

15 new weapon/elemental/holy effect transients: original Dungeon Crush sound design,
CC0. Recipe: tools/render-combat-audio.py (noise, filtered swishes, impacts and chimes).
These are sound effects; no new music or speech was composed/synthesized.
Exact packaged SHA256s, original archive checksums, entries and processing details:
combat-audio-sources.json. No runtime network access is used to play these sounds.
